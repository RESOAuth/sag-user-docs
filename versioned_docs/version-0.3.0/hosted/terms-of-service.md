# Terms of service for the hosted gateway

Last updated: 24 September 2026.

These terms cover the free shared sign-in service RESOAuth Ltd runs at
`auth.resoauth.cloud`. They do not change the licence for the open-source SAG
software you can run yourself. A separate agreement with RESOAuth takes
precedence if you have one.

## Free service, with no guarantee

You can connect an application to the shared gateway without paying us. We
work to keep it available and secure, but we do not promise that it will
always be available, uninterrupted, or suitable for a particular purpose.
We may change, limit, or stop the free service.

Your application remains responsible for checking the tokens it receives and
for deciding what a signed-in person may do. See
[Tokens and claims](./tokens-and-claims.md).

## Use it fairly

Use the gateway lawfully. Do not attack it, try to bypass its security or
rate limits, or send traffic that prevents other people from signing in.

We may slow down or pause traffic to protect the shared service. This may
happen if your application opens a large number of connections or more than
100 people sign in through it in an hour. People signing in may see a message
asking them to wait. The figure is a guide to when we may act, not a promise
of capacity or an automatic cut-off.

If you expect that level of use, [get in touch](../support/index.md) so we can
discuss a dedicated instance or other arrangements.
